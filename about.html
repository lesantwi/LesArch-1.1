About LesArch

Our mission to preserve and share history through technology.

Our Story

LesArch began with a simple yet profound idea: to make the rich tapestry of human history more accessible and engaging than ever before. Born from a passion for archaeology and a belief in the power of digital innovation, our platform strives to bridge the gap between academic research and public curiosity. We aim to bring the wonders of ancient sites directly to your screen, fostering a deeper connection with our shared past.

Our journey involves meticulous research, cutting-edge 3D reconstruction techniques, and a commitment to accuracy. We collaborate with archaeologists, historians, and digital specialists to ensure that every site presented on LesArch is not only visually stunning but also historically sound.

Our Vision

We envision a future where archaeological heritage is universally accessible, understood, and appreciated. LesArch is built on three core pillars:

Global Accessibility

Breaking down geographical barriers to allow anyone, anywhere, to explore archaeological sites and their contexts.

Immersive Learning

Utilizing interactive 3D models and rich narratives to provide an engaging and educational experience beyond traditional methods.

Preservation & Advocacy

Raising awareness about threatened archaeological sites and promoting their preservation through digital documentation and public engagement.

Our Commitment to Open Data & Research

LesArch is dedicated to fostering open science and supporting the archaeological research community. We believe that digital archaeological data, especially 3D models, should be findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable (FAIR principles).

While LesArch provides an interactive visualization platform, we strongly encourage the deposition of underlying research data in established, long-term archaeological repositories. We recommend researchers contributing models to LesArch to also publish their data in platforms such as:

  • iDAI.repo (German Archaeological Institute) - Ideal for archaeological research data.
  • Zenodo - A general-purpose open-access repository that provides DOIs for citable research outputs.
  • ARIADNE Infrastructure - The central portal for archaeological data in Europe, which often aggregates data from other repositories.

By doing so, we ensure that the valuable data behind our visualizations is preserved, discoverable, and citable for future academic endeavors, contributing to the broader archaeological discourse and preservation efforts.
